2. Data we collect

  • Client and company data — company name, registration details, addresses, and documents required for CIDB licensing, Green Card registration, worker permit and related applications.
  • Identity documents — where required for an application you have engaged us for, copies of identity documents such as identity cards, passports and work permits of directors, personnel or workers, supplied by you or your employer.
  • Contact details — names, phone numbers and email addresses of clients, their representatives, and our sales agents.
  • Platform usage data — account and activity records needed to operate and secure our platform.

3. How we use personal data

  • To prepare, submit and manage applications and services you have engaged us for (e.g. CIDB licences, Green Card registration, worker permits).
  • To issue quotations, invoices and receipts, and to maintain accounting records.
  • To communicate about your cases, including automated service notifications.
  • To operate, secure and improve our platform.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. WhatsApp notifications

We send service notifications and documents (for example, licence and invoice documents) to our registered agents and clients through the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. When we do so, the recipient's phone number and the message content are processed by Meta in order to deliver the message, subject to Meta's own terms and privacy policy. Our WhatsApp number is an automated sender; replies to it are not monitored. If you prefer not to receive WhatsApp notifications, contact us and we will use another channel.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as needed to deliver our services:

  • Government bodies and regulators — e.g. CIDB and other Malaysian authorities, as required for the applications you engage us for.
  • Service providers (processors) — providers of cloud hosting, file storage, messaging (including Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform) and similar infrastructure that we use to run our services, bound to process data only on our instructions.
  • Professional advisers and authorities — where required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting and regulatory requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls on our platform and encrypted transmission. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

8. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you may request access to or correction of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent to processing, subject to legal and contractual limits. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date above.
