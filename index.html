COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What education requirements apply to each CIDB grade?

G1, G2 and G3 do not require a diploma. Based on the reference chart, G4 and G5 require 1 Diploma; G6 and G7 require 1 Degree + 1 Diploma; and Foreign G7 requires 2 Degrees. Requirements may depend on the applicable registration category and current regulations.

What is the minimum bank statement or paid-up capital listed for each grade?

The supplied chart lists G1: RM5,000; G2: RM25,000; G3: RM50,000; G4: RM150,000; G5: RM250,000; G6: RM500,000; G7: RM750,000; and Foreign G7: RM750,000. Please confirm which form of capital evidence is accepted for your application.

What is the tender project limit for each CIDB grade?

The supplied chart lists G1 up to RM200,000; G2 up to RM500,000; G3 up to RM1 million; G4 up to RM3 million; G5 up to RM5 million; and G6 up to RM10 million. G7 and Foreign G7 are listed as having no limit. Confirm the current applicable limits before tendering.

What do Malaysian citizens need to apply for a CIDB Green Card?

For Malaysian citizens, a copy of your Malaysian identity card (IC) is all that is needed to get started. No training course is required, based on the information provided by ADICCA Group. Contact us if you need help with the application.

Can a foreign worker apply for a CIDB Green Card?

Eligibility depends on your work permit. Please provide your work permit for our team to check whether you are eligible to apply. We will advise you on the next steps after reviewing it.

Can a foreign-owned company apply for CIDB registration?

Foreign-owned companies may be subject to specific registration categories, grade restrictions, capital and technical personnel requirements. Contact ADICCA Group for an eligibility assessment based on the applicable rules.

What documents should I prepare before applying?

The required documents depend on your company and application type. The reference chart mentions bank statements or paid-up capital and education qualifications. Contact our team to confirm the supporting documents applicable to your case before submission.

Can ADICCA assist with CIDB registration, renewals and grade upgrades?

ADICCA Group assists with eligible CIDB contractor registration, renewals and grade upgrades. Contact us to discuss your company’s current grade, intended grade and next steps.
